# Social Media Strategy

## Purpose
This skill provides a framework for designing high-performing Instagram carousels that guide users through a narrative journey. It transforms product features into compelling visual stories that drive both community engagement and direct conversions.

## Instructions
1. **Define the Hook (Slide 1):** Create a high-impact headline that addresses a specific pain point or desired outcome related to the Ashwagandha and Gotu Kola face cream.
2. **The Bridge (Slide 2):** Introduce the core problem (e.g., stressed, aging, or inflamed skin) and hint at the botanical solution.
3. **The Deep Dive (Slides 3-4):** Break down the specific benefits of the key ingredients—Ashwagandha for stress-resistance and Gotu Kola for healing/collagen—using concise, punchy bullet points.
4. **The Transformation (Slide 5):** Describe the sensory experience (texture, scent) and the visible results users can expect after application.
5. **The Conversion (Slide 6):** End with a clear, single Call to Action (CTA) such as "Shop the Glow" or "Save for your skincare routine."
6. **Visual Synthesis:** For every slide, generate a descriptive AI image prompt that ensures a consistent aesthetic, focusing on "Clean Beauty" photography styles.
7. **Caption Crafting:** Write a supporting caption that expands on the carousel's value proposition and includes a mix of branded and niche hashtags.

## Key Principles
- **The 3-Second Rule:** The first slide must stop the scroll with a bold claim or a relatable question.
- **Information Hierarchy:** Use "Bite-Sized" text; no more than 25 words per slide to ensure readability on mobile screens.
- **Visual Continuity:** Maintain a consistent color palette and lighting style across all image prompts to build brand recognition.

## Output Format
- **Slide 1-6 Breakdown:** 
    - **Header:** [Slide Title/Hook]
    - **On-Slide Copy:** [Exact text to appear on the graphic]
    - **Image Prompt:** [Detailed prompt for AI image generation]
- **Final Instagram Caption:** [Engaging caption text]
- **Hashtag Set:** [5-10 relevant hashtags]
