# Trend Analysis

## Purpose
This skill enables the agent to dissect high-performing skincare content and extract the specific visual and psychological triggers that drive engagement. It bridges the gap between raw social media data and high-converting creative assets for Bare and Fair’s Barrier Repair Face Cream.

## Instructions
1. **Audit Viral Content:** Search Instagram Reels and TikTok for "barrier repair," "skin barrier," and "soothing skincare" to identify posts with high engagement-to-follower ratios from the last 30–60 days.
2. **Deconstruct Visual Hooks:** Identify the first 3 seconds of top-performing videos—specifically looking for macro texture shots, "satisfying" product scoops, or immediate redness-reduction transitions.
3. **Analyze Sentiment & Language:** Extract high-frequency phrases and emotional pain points from the comments sections (e.g., "my skin is screaming," "glass skin goals," "doesn't pill under makeup").
4. **Identify Audio & Format Patterns:** Note recurring audio trends (ASMR, educational voiceovers, or lo-fi beats) and formats (e.g., "Get Ready With Me" vs. "Product Science").
5. **Translate to Creative Strategy:** Map these findings to Bare and Fair’s specific product benefits, prioritizing sensory-focused elements that align with current user preferences.

## Key Principles
- **Sensory Dominance:** Prioritize "visual haptics"—content that allows the viewer to "feel" the texture of the Barrier Repair Face Cream through the screen.
- **Data-Backed Creativity:** Every creative recommendation must be linked to a specific observed trend or high-performing metric.
- **Brand Synthesis:** Ensure viral trends are filtered through Bare and Fair’s brand voice to maintain authenticity and avoid "trend-chasing" that feels out of character.

## Output Format
A "Trend-to-Creative" Strategy Report including:
- **Trend Summary:** The top 3 recurring themes currently dominating the skincare niche.
- **Visual Shot List:** 3–5 specific scenes (e.g., macro cream swirl, application on damp skin).
- **Hook & Copy Angles:** 3 high-converting hook options based on current audience sentiment.
- **Sensory Direction:** Specific instructions for lighting, sound (ASMR vs. Music), and pacing to maximize the "sensory-focused" objective.
