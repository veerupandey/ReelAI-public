# Copywriting

## Purpose
This skill enables the agent to generate high-conversion social media assets by blending psychological triggers with product benefits. It focuses on creating cohesive, multi-slide narratives that drive engagement and brand authority for skincare products.

## Instructions
1. **Research & Positioning**: Identify the core benefits of Ashwagandha (adaptogenic, anti-inflammatory) and Gotu Kola (collagen boosting, skin repair) to establish a "calm and renew" narrative.
2. **Carousel Structure**: Organize the 6-slide sequence to follow a logical flow:
    - Slide 1: The Hook (Address a specific skin pain point).
    - Slide 2: The Problem (Explain why traditional solutions fail).
    - Slide 3: The Solution (Introduce the Ashwagandha & Gotu Kola cream).
    - Slide 4: Key Ingredient Deep Dive (Highlight the science/benefits).
    - Slide 5: Social Proof/Results (Describe the transformation).
    - Slide 6: Call to Action (Direct the user to the link in bio or shop).
3. **Visual Conceptualization**: For each slide, write a detailed image generation prompt focusing on a "clean beauty" aesthetic—using soft lighting, natural textures, and botanical elements.
4. **Caption Crafting**: Write a final Instagram caption that expands on the carousel's value, using a mix of short and long sentences to maintain readability.
5. **Refinement**: Ensure the tone is premium, soothing, and trustworthy.

## Key Principles
- **Benefit-Driven Copy**: Focus on the "glow" and "calm" the user achieves rather than just the chemical composition of the ingredients.
- **Visual-Text Harmony**: Ensure the text on the slide is minimal (under 20 words) and works in tandem with the described image prompt.
- **The "Scroll-Stop" Factor**: Use Slide 1 to trigger an emotional response or curiosity within the first second of viewing.

## Output Format
- **Slide 1-6**: 
    - [Slide Text: Headline and Sub-headline]
    - [Image Prompt: Detailed description for an AI image generator]
- **Instagram Caption**: 
    - [Hook line]
    - [Body copy with emojis]
    - [Clear Call-to-Action]
    - [Relevant Hashtag Set]
