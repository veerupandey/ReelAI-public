# Social Media Marketing

## Purpose
This skill enables the agent to analyze high-performing short-form video trends and translate them into persuasive, platform-specific marketing assets that drive engagement and conversions for skincare brands.

## Instructions
1. **Trend Audit:** Research current skincare-specific trends on Instagram and TikTok, focusing on "Skinfluencer" content, "Get Ready With Me" (GRWM) formats, and educational "Skin Barrier" explainers.
2. **Competitor Benchmarking:** Analyze top-performing content from leading skincare brands to identify successful visual styles, color palettes, and pacing.
3. **Hook Development:** Create three high-impact hooks (visual or verbal) designed to stop the scroll within the first 1.5 seconds.
4. **Scripting:** Draft a 30-second script for Bare & Fair's Barrier Repair Face Cream that follows the "Problem-Agitation-Solution" framework, emphasizing the "damaged barrier" pain point.
5. **Visual Storyboarding:** Define specific visual cues, including B-roll shots (texture shots, application), text overlays, and transition points that align with current algorithm preferences for high retention.
6. **Call to Action (CTA):** Craft a platform-native CTA that encourages "Saves" or "Shares," as these are high-value algorithm triggers.

## Key Principles
- **The 3-Second Rule:** The value proposition or the "hook" must be established immediately to prevent scrolling.
- **UGC Aesthetic:** Prioritize "User Generated Content" styles that feel authentic and relatable rather than overly polished commercial productions.
- **Education-tainment:** Blend scientific facts about barrier repair with entertaining visual transitions to maintain viewer interest.
- **Retention-Focused Editing:** Use frequent cuts, text-on-screen, and dynamic movement to keep the average view time high.

## Output Format
- **Trend Summary:** A brief overview of the specific viral trend being leveraged.
- **The Script:** A two-column table (Time/Visual vs. Audio/Dialogue).
- **Visual Concept:** Description of the lighting, setting, and "Bare & Fair" brand aesthetic.
- **Engagement Strategy:** Recommended trending audio types, 3-5 niche hashtags, and the primary CTA.
