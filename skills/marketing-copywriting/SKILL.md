# Marketing Copywriting

## Purpose
This skill enables the agent to transform competitive market research and viral trends into high-converting promotional text. It focuses on bridging the gap between consumer psychology and brand-specific value propositions to drive measurable engagement.

## Instructions
1. **Analyze Viral Insights:** Review identified strategies from top skincare brands, noting successful hooks (e.g., "POV," "Skin School," or "Before/After" narratives).
2. **Define the USP:** Isolate the specific benefits of Bare and Fair’s Barrier Repair Face Cream, such as ceramide content, soothing redness, or clinical results.
3. **Draft the Hook:** Create 3-5 "scroll-stopping" opening lines that address a specific pain point (e.g., "Stop stripping your skin barrier") or a desired outcome (e.g., "The secret to glass skin starts with repair").
4. **Apply Copy Frameworks:** Use the PAS (Problem-Agitation-Solution) or AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action) framework to structure the body copy.
5. **Optimize for Instagram:** Format the text with line breaks for readability, use relevant emojis to add personality, and ensure the tone is community-focused rather than overly corporate.
6. **Insert Call to Action (CTA):** Provide a clear, low-friction instruction (e.g., "Tap to heal your skin," "Shop the Barrier Repair Cream now").

## Key Principles
- **Show, Don't Just Tell:** Use sensory language to describe the texture and feel of the cream on the skin.
- **Platform Native:** Ensure the copy sounds like a recommendation from a trusted friend or expert, fitting the aesthetic of an Instagram feed or Story.
- **Benefit-Centric:** Focus on the "transformation" (healthy, glowing skin) rather than just the technical ingredient list.

## Output Format
- **Campaign Angle:** A brief description of the psychological trigger used.
- **Hook Options:** 3 distinct opening lines.
- **Primary Body Copy:** The main ad text formatted for Instagram.
- **Suggested Visual Direction:** A brief note on what type of imagery/video should accompany the text (e.g., "Close-up texture shot" or "User testimonial").
- **CTA & Hashtags:** Final action button text and a curated set of 5-7 relevant hashtags.
