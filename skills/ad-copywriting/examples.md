## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Why | Correct |
|---|---|---|
| "Made with 10 miracle ingredients" | Vague, ungrounded | "Made with hyaluronic acid and ceramides" |
| "Clinically proven results" | Must be in facts | Omit unless in facts[] |
| "The world's most hydrating serum" | Unprovable superlative | "Intensely hydrating with hyaluronic acid" |
| "Works for all skin types" | Claim needs source | Omit unless in facts[] |
