# Creative Copywriting

## Purpose
This skill enables the agent to synthesize market trends and product benefits into high-impact, emotionally resonant advertising scripts. It focuses on transforming technical skincare specifications into sensory experiences that drive engagement and sales on short-form video platforms.

## Instructions
1. **Trend Synthesis:** Research current viral skincare movements (e.g., "skin barrier repair," "glass skin," or "minimalist routines") to identify high-performing keywords and visual styles.
2. **Problem/Solution Mapping:** Identify the specific pain points Bare & Fair's Barrier Repair Face Cream addresses, such as redness, stinging, or environmental damage.
3. **Drafting the Hook:** Create three high-tension opening lines (0-3 seconds) that address a relatable skincare struggle or offer a surprising visual hook.
4. **Sensory Layering:** Infuse the script with descriptive language that evokes the product’s texture (e.g., "cloud-like," "velvety," "weightless") and the physical sensation of relief.
5. **Visual Storyboarding:** Pair every line of dialogue with a specific visual instruction (e.g., "Close-up of cream texture," "Transition from irritated skin to glowing finish").
6. **Conversion Optimization:** End with a single, clear Call to Action (CTA) optimized for Instagram’s interface.

## Key Principles
- **Show, Don't Just Tell:** Use sensory adjectives to make the viewer "feel" the product through the screen.
- **The 3-Second Rule:** Capture attention immediately by addressing a specific skin "villain" (e.g., "Stop stripping your skin!").
- **Authenticity over Polish:** Design the script to feel like a peer-to-peer recommendation rather than a corporate broadcast.

## Output Format
A structured Instagram Reels Campaign Brief including:
- **Trend Context:** A brief explanation of the viral trend being leveraged.
- **The Script:** A two-column table (Visual/Audio) with timestamps for a 30-second duration.
- **Visual Concept:** Description of the lighting, color palette, and "vibe" (e.g., "Clean Girl Aesthetic" or "Clinical/Science-Forward").
- **Primary CTA:** The final text overlay and caption recommendation.
