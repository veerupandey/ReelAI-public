# Caption Design — Style Examples

## Style A — Dark Bar
```
Background: black bar, 65% opacity, full width, 120px tall
Text: white, Helvetica Bold 52pt, centered on bar
Position: lower third at y = H × 0.78
Shadow: 2px offset in all 4 diagonal directions, black 80% opacity
```
Best for: High-energy, TikTok, dark-aesthetic products

## Style B — Brand Color
```
Background: transparent
Text: brand_hex (extracted from product image dominant color)
Position: lower third at y = H × 0.78
Shadow: 2px diagonal shadow, black 80% opacity (makes text readable on any background)
```
Best for: Luxury, earthy organic, minimalist brands

## Style C — Tinted Bar
```
Background: tint_hex bar (lightened brand color), 75% opacity
Text: #1a1a1a (near-black), Helvetica Bold 52pt
Position: lower third at y = H × 0.78
```
Best for: Clean, editorial, aspirational brands

## What NOT to caption

```
# Too long — full sentences
"Your skin deserves the best ingredients nature has to offer."

# Should become
["Best Ingredients", "From Nature"]
```
